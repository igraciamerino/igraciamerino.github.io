My research focuses on the development of formal methods and control-theoretic tools for the analysis, design, and verification of autonomous systems.

My broader goal is to enable autonomous and robotic systems that are both **safe and capable**, providing rigorous guarantees while retaining the computational efficiency required for practical applications.

## Research Areas

### Formal Methods for Autonomous Systems

I develop formal verification and synthesis methods for dynamical and stochastic systems, with an emphasis on guarantees that remain meaningful under uncertainty and model imperfections.

### Data-Driven and Stochastic Systems

I study methods that incorporate data into the formal analysis of uncertain dynamical systems, including approaches based on distributional uncertainty and statistical guarantees.

### Motion Planning and Robotics

I develop motion-planning methods for high-dimensional systems that leverage formal guarantees while remaining computationally efficient enough for robotic applications.

### Control and Verification

My work combines control theory and formal methods to analyze safety and performance properties of autonomous systems and to develop controllers that satisfy such properties.
